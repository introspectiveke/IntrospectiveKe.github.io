Where the Science Is Going

Life science is moving from the bench to the dataset. These are the shifts my work connects to, and the part of each that I handle.

Biology is data-driven

Bench results now arrive as tables, sequences and images that need code to interpret. My part: cleaning, statistics and visualization with pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

Sequence data keeps growing

Manual, step-by-step analysis does not scale with the volume of genomic data. My part: scripted sequence analysis and BLAST automation with Biopython.

Machine learning enters the lab and the field

Predictive models are becoming a standard tool in life science and agriculture. My part: models for agricultural and health data with Scikit-learn, Keras and PyTorch.

Reproducibility is expected

Results need documented steps and checks that others can repeat. My part: versioned notebooks, written-up methods and the quality-control habits from laboratory work.
