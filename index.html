About Me

I’m a biochemist who works with biological and agricultural data in Python. My focus is data cleaning, statistical analysis, sequence analysis and machine learning, with figures and notebooks that others can reproduce.

I hold a BSc in Biochemistry from Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology (MMUST). My training started in the laboratory: at the Kenya Plant Health Inspectorate Service (KEPHIS) I worked on laboratory quality assurance, compliance data collection and scientific documentation (March – September 2023).

In 2023 I also competed on Zindi, building and deploying predictive models in Python, Scikit-learn and Keras on African agricultural and health datasets (May – December 2023).
